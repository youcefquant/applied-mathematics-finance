💡 Financial Explanation and Simplification (Example: Game Cards 🎮):
Let's explain the previous sentence in the simplest way possible so you can remember it or include it in your explanation: 
1. "calculate individual asset values" (Calculate the value of each asset individually). Financial Meaning:
2. Before you combine anything, you must know the current market value of each item you own.
3.  Example: You own 10 FIFA NARD cards (this is the first asset), and the current market price of one card is $100. Therefore, their total individual value is $1,000.

4. 2. Determine their total weight. Financial meaning:
   3.  How much of your total wealth do these assets represent? Have you put all your money into them or diversified your holdings?
   4.  Example: Besides the cards, you decided to put $500 in cash in your piggy bank (this is a safe asset). So your total wealth is now:
   5.  $1,000 (value of the cards) + $500 (cash) = $1,500. Weight:
   6.  The cards represent two-thirds of your wealth (about 66%), and the cash represents one-third (33%).
